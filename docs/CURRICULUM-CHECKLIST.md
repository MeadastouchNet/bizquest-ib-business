# BizQuest curriculum checklist

What BizQuest teaches, topic by topic, to tick off against the official **IB Business Management guide (first
assessment 2024)**. Everything was written from memory, so nothing here has been checked against the guide yet.

How to use it: tick each line that matches the guide. Note anything in the guide that is **missing** here, and
anything here that is **not** in the guide (it may be from the older guide). HL = higher level only.

## Unit 1: Introduction to business management

- [ ] **1.1 Introduction to business management:** what a business is (human, physical, financial resources, entrepreneurship); the four business functions; primary, secondary, tertiary, quaternary sectors; sectoral change; entrepreneurship and intrapreneurship *(intrapreneurship: is it in the 2024 guide?)*; reasons for starting a business; problems new businesses face
- [ ] **1.2 Types of business entities:** private vs public sector; sole traders; partnerships; privately held companies; publicly held companies; for-profit social enterprises (incl. cooperatives); non-profit social enterprises (incl. NGOs)
- [ ] **1.3 Business objectives:** vision and mission statements; growth, profit, protecting shareholder value, ethical objectives; strategic and tactical objectives; corporate social responsibility (CSR); SMART objectives and why objectives change *(are these two in the 2024 guide?)*
- [ ] **1.4 Stakeholders:** internal and external stakeholders; their interests; conflict between stakeholders; stakeholder mapping (toolkit)
- [ ] **1.5 Growth and evolution:** internal and external economies and diseconomies of scale; reasons to grow or stay small; internal (organic) growth; external growth: mergers and acquisitions, takeovers, joint ventures, strategic alliances, franchising
- [ ] **1.6 Multinational companies (MNCs):** what an MNC is; reasons for becoming one; impact on host countries

## Unit 2: Human resource management

- [ ] **2.1 Introduction to HRM:** role of HRM; workforce planning; labour turnover (formula); internal and external factors affecting HR planning (demographics, labour mobility, technology, teleworking, flexitime, gig economy, laws); recruitment (internal/external); training (induction, on-the-job, off-the-job; cognitive and behavioural); appraisal (formative, summative, 360-degree, self); dismissal and redundancy; outsourcing, offshoring, reshoring *(check where the guide puts recruitment, training and appraisal)*
- [ ] **2.1 (HL):** reasons for resistance to change; HR strategies to reduce it
- [ ] **2.2 Organizational structure:** delegation, span of control, levels of hierarchy, chain of command, centralization and decentralization, delayering, bureaucracy; flat and tall structures; structures by function, product, region
- [ ] **2.2 (HL):** matrix structure; project-based organization *(Handy's shamrock left out: check)*
- [ ] **2.3 Leadership and management:** functions of management; management vs leadership; autocratic, paternalistic, democratic, laissez-faire, situational leadership
- [ ] **2.4 Motivation and demotivation:** Taylor, Maslow, Herzberg; financial rewards (salary, wages: time and piece rates, commission, profit-related pay, performance-related pay, employee share ownership, fringe payments); non-financial rewards (job enrichment, enlargement, rotation, empowerment, purpose, teamwork)
- [ ] **2.4 (HL):** McClelland; Deci and Ryan (self-determination theory); Adams (equity theory); Vroom (expectancy theory)
- [ ] **2.5 Organizational culture (HL):** meaning and types of culture; culture clashes; how individuals and culture influence each other
- [ ] **2.6 Communication:** formal and informal; methods; barriers and how to overcome them
- [ ] **2.7 Industrial/employee relations (HL):** employee and employer representatives; sources of conflict; employee actions (collective bargaining, work-to-rule, go-slow, overtime ban, strike); employer actions (threats, changes to contracts, lockout, closure); resolution (conciliation, arbitration, employee participation, no-strike and single-union agreements)

## Unit 3: Finance and accounts

- [ ] **3.1 Introduction to finance:** role of finance; capital vs revenue expenditure
- [ ] **3.2 Sources of finance:** internal (retained profit, sale of assets, personal funds) and external (share capital, loans, overdrafts, trade credit, leasing, crowdfunding, microfinance, business angels); short-term vs long-term; choosing a source
- [ ] **3.3 Costs and revenues:** fixed, variable, semi-variable; direct and indirect; total cost, total revenue, revenue streams
- [ ] **3.4 Final accounts:** profit and loss account; balance sheet; intangible assets; users of accounts
- [ ] **3.4 (HL):** depreciation: straight-line and units of production
- [ ] **3.5 Profitability and liquidity ratios:** gross profit margin, profit margin, ROCE, current ratio, acid-test ratio
- [ ] **3.6 Debt/efficiency ratios (HL):** stock turnover, debtor days, creditor days, gearing; insolvency vs bankruptcy
- [ ] **3.7 Cash flow:** profit vs cash; working capital; cash flow forecasts; strategies for cash flow problems
- [ ] **3.8 Investment appraisal:** payback period, average rate of return
- [ ] **3.8 (HL):** net present value
- [ ] **3.9 Budgets (HL):** budgets; cost and profit centres; variance analysis

## Unit 4: Marketing

- [ ] **4.1 Introduction to marketing:** marketing and other functions; goods vs services; market vs product orientation; market size, growth and share; market leadership *(social marketing left out: check)*
- [ ] **4.2 Marketing planning:** marketing plan; segmentation (demographic, geographic, psychographic, behavioural); targeting, niche vs mass; positioning and perception maps; USP and differentiation; Ansoff matrix (toolkit)
- [ ] **4.3 Sales forecasting (HL):** three-part and four-part centred moving averages; trend; seasonal, cyclical and random variation; forecasting; benefits and limitations *(which moving averages are required?)*
- [ ] **4.4 Market research:** primary (surveys, interviews, focus groups, observation) and secondary research; qualitative vs quantitative; sampling (random, stratified, quota, cluster, convenience, snowball); ethics
- [ ] **4.5 The seven Ps:** product life cycle, extension strategies, BCG matrix (toolkit), branding, packaging; pricing (cost-plus, penetration, skimming, psychological, loss leader, price discrimination, dynamic, competitive); promotion (above and below the line, promotional mix, social media, guerrilla); place (channels, intermediaries, e-commerce); people, processes, physical evidence
- [ ] **4.6 International marketing (HL):** opportunities; entry methods (exporting, franchising/licensing, joint ventures, alliances, M&A, direct investment); barriers (cultural, legal, political, economic, practical); standardization vs adaptation, glocalization

## Unit 5: Operations management

- [ ] **5.1 Introduction to operations management:** role of operations; goods and services; economic, social and environmental sustainability
- [ ] **5.2 Operations methods:** job, batch, mass/flow, mass customization
- [ ] **5.3 Lean and quality management (HL):** lean production; kaizen, JIT, kanban, andon; cradle to cradle; quality control vs quality assurance; TQM, quality circles, benchmarking; quality standards (ISO)
- [ ] **5.4 Location:** location factors; outsourcing/subcontracting, offshoring, insourcing, reshoring
- [ ] **5.5 Break-even analysis:** contribution; break-even quantity and revenue; break-even chart; margin of safety; target profit output; target price; effects of changes; limitations
- [ ] **5.6 Production planning (HL):** supply chains; JIT vs JIC; stock control charts; capacity utilization; productivity; defect rate; cost to buy vs cost to make
- [ ] **5.7 Crisis management and contingency planning (HL):** crisis management vs contingency planning; transparency, communication, speed, control; costs and benefits of planning
- [ ] **5.8 Research and development (HL):** importance of R&D; product, process, positioning and paradigm innovation; incremental vs disruptive innovation
- [ ] **5.9 Management information systems (HL):** data, databases, analytics; cybersecurity and cybercrime; AI, big data, cloud, IoT, VR; data mining; loyalty programmes; employee monitoring / digital Taylorism; ethics *(check the exact technology list)*
