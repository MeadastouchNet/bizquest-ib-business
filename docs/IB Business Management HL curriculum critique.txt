# IB Business Management HL curriculum audit

Corrections needed

Your BizQuest checklist is broadly accurate in its coverage of the 2024 IB Business Management HL syllabus, but it is not yet complete or fully aligned with the official guide.

I compared your uploaded `CURRICULUM-CHECKLIST.md` against the IB's Business Management Guide — First Assessment 2024, including the syllabus content, prescribed Business Management Toolkit, and HL requirements.

The checklist correctly identifies all 37 syllabus topics across Units 1–5, and most of the major subject areas are represented. However, there are several substantive omissions, incorrect HL classifications, and topics carried over from the previous syllabus.

### Sources and scope

My primary reference is the [official IB Business Management Guide (PDF)](https://www.ibo.org/globalassets/new-structure/university-admission/pdfs/subject-guides/business-management-guide.pdf?utm_source=chatgpt.com), particularly syllabus pages 23–36 and toolkit pages 14–15.

That publicly hosted IB PDF was updated in May 2022. I also checked the July 2024 revision, which corrected Paper 3 wording and percentage signs in formulae without changing the prescribed syllabus topics.&#x20;

## 3. Business Management Toolkit — full verification

The guide requires 15 prescribed tools, divided into eight tools for SL/HL and seven HL-only tools.

Your checklist includes or references 13 of the 15. The two genuinely missing tools are Porter and Hofstede.

SL AND HL — 8 TOOLS

SWOT analysis  Ansoff matrix  STEEPLE analysis  BCG matrix  Business plan  Decision trees  Descriptive statistics  Circular business models

HL ONLY — 7 TOOLS

Force field analysis  Gantt charts  Hofstede's cultural dimensions  Porter's generic strategies  Contribution  Critical path analysis  Simple linear regression

Two classification corrections: your toolkit lines 6.4 and 6.5 should explicitly mark force field analysis and Gantt charts as HL-only.

There are also three toolkit detail gaps worth addressing.

Critical path analysis (HL): The guide requires completing and analysing diagrams, identifying the critical path, and calculating free float and total float. Students are not required to draw an entire critical path diagram from scratch.

Contribution (HL): Treat this as its own toolkit item covering make-or-buy analysis, contribution costing and absorption costing. Basic contribution in break-even analysis remains part of 5.5 for SL and HL.

Descriptive statistics (SL/HL): Add explicit coverage of mean, mode, median, bar charts, pie charts, infographics, quartiles and standard deviation. Teaching it only lightly in 4.4 is insufficient for the toolkit requirements.

For simple linear regression, the prescribed elements are scatter diagrams, line of best fit, correlation and extrapolation. The guide does not prescribe calculating Pearson's correlation coefficient \\(r\\).&#x20;

## 4. Cross-cutting curriculum elements missing from the checklist

Because this is a teaching-coverage checklist rather than just a list of chapter headings, I would add a small separate section for the following.

Four key concepts: Creativity, Change, Ethics and Sustainability. These should be integrated across all five units, rather than taught exclusively as isolated topics.

HL Paper 3 preparation: Analysis of social enterprises, identifying human needs and organizational challenges, and writing justified strategic recommendations. The July 2024 guide specifies three compulsory questions, worth 2, 6 and 17 marks.

Internal assessment preparation: A business research project addressing a real organization's issue or problem through one of the four key conceptual lenses, with a maximum of 1,800 words.&#x20;

## Final assessment

The document is a solid starting point. Its five-unit structure and topic numbering are correct, and it already includes the majority of prescribed material.

The most significant weakness is not missing whole syllabus sections. It is that several HL learning outcomes are hidden inside broad descriptions, certain toolkit requirements are incomplete, and some older-syllabus content is presented alongside compulsory 2024 material.

For BizQuest, I recommend distinguishing each curriculum item as either IB-prescribed, IB-prescribed HL-only, or supplementary/enrichment. That will make the checklist substantially more reliable for measuring actual curriculum coverage, especially when planning revision and exam preparation.

The official guide should be the definitive baseline, with its prescribed learning outcomes and assessment-objective levels controlling what counts as complete.
