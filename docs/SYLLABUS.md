# Syllabus: what each built unit covers

IB Business Management guide, first assessment 2024. Since 2026-10-26 the content has been corrected against an
external review of the official guide ([the curriculum critique](IB%20Business%20Management%20HL%20curriculum%20critique.txt),
which compared BizQuest with the guide's syllabus pages 23–36 and toolkit pages 14–15). That review is the source for
the labels below; nobody has yet ticked the content off against the guide itself.

Each content point has one of three labels, which match how the app shows them:

| Label | Meaning | In the app |
|---|---|---|
| **SL/HL** | Prescribed for all students | Normal content |
| **HL** | Prescribed for HL only | `HL` tag on the card, quiz question or lab |
| **Extra** | Useful, but not prescribed in the 2024 guide (older syllabus, or enrichment) | A dashed "➕ Beyond the IB syllabus" box (`<div class="extra">`); never the subject of a quiz question |

## Unit 1: Introduction to business management

| Topic | Content points | Label |
|---|---|---|
| 1.1 Introduction to business management | What a business is (human, physical, financial resources and entrepreneurship); the four business functions; primary, secondary, tertiary and quaternary sectors; sectoral change; entrepreneurship; reasons for starting a business; problems a new business may face | SL/HL |
| 1.1 | Intrapreneurship | Extra |
| 1.2 Types of business entities | Private vs public sector; sole traders; partnerships; privately held and publicly held companies; for-profit social enterprises (private sector companies, public sector companies, cooperatives); non-profit social enterprises incl. NGOs | SL/HL |
| 1.3 Business objectives | Vision and mission statements; growth, profit, protecting shareholder value, ethical objectives; strategic and tactical objectives; CSR | SL/HL |
| 1.3 | SMART objectives | Extra |
| 1.3 | Why objectives change | A suggested inquiry question in the guide, not a content point: kept as a normal card |
| 1.4 Stakeholders | Internal and external stakeholders; their interests; conflict between stakeholders | SL/HL |
| 1.4 | Stakeholder mapping (not one of the 15 toolkit tools) | Extra |
| 1.5 Growth and evolution | Economies and diseconomies of scale; reasons to grow or stay small; internal growth; external growth: mergers and acquisitions, takeovers, joint ventures, strategic alliances, franchising | SL/HL |
| 1.6 Multinational companies (MNCs) | What an MNC is; the impact of MNCs on host countries | SL/HL |
| 1.6 | Reasons for becoming an MNC (background for the impact) | Extra (noted in the card) |

## The business management toolkit (BizQuest "Unit 6")

The guide doesn't number the toolkit as a unit; BizQuest gives it id `6` so it gets its own lessons, Boss Battle and
formula sheet. The guide prescribes 15 tools: 8 for SL and HL, 7 for HL only. All 15 are taught.

| Tool | Where | Label |
|---|---|---|
| SWOT analysis | 6.1 | SL/HL |
| STEEPLE analysis | 6.2 | SL/HL |
| Decision trees | 6.3 | SL/HL |
| Circular business models | 6.8 | SL/HL |
| Business plan | 6.9 | SL/HL |
| Descriptive statistics: mean, median, mode, quartiles, standard deviation, bar charts, pie charts, infographics | 6.10 | SL/HL |
| Ansoff matrix | 4.2 | SL/HL |
| BCG matrix | 4.5 | SL/HL |
| Force field analysis | 6.4 | HL |
| Gantt charts | 6.5 | HL |
| Critical path analysis: complete and analyse networks; EST, LFT, total float and free float; the critical path | 6.6 | HL |
| Simple linear regression: scatter diagrams, line of best fit, correlation (interpreted, r not calculated), extrapolation | 6.7 | HL |
| Porter's generic strategies | 6.11 | HL |
| Hofstede's cultural dimensions | 6.12 | HL |
| Contribution: make-or-buy, contribution costing, absorption costing | 6.13 (basic contribution also in 5.5) | HL |
